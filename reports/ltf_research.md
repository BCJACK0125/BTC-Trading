## Why: adverse selection

| limit | would fill | avg R if filled | avg R if never pulled back | share of total R never pulled back |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| -1 ATR | 17.5% | -0.35 | 0.54 | 115.9% |

The trades that run without looking back carry most of the edge; a limit order only fills the weaker ones, and a tighter 1h stop is hit more often by noise.
